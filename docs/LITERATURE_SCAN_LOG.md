# Scan log
 
## 2026-09-18
- AbRank / WALLE-Affinity (arXiv 2506.17857, code: github.com/biochunan/AbRank-WALLE-Affinity) — bucket 1+2 — 380k Ab-Ag binding assays across 9 sources incl. SKEMPI-v2, homology-clustered splits (75% seq-id), ranking beats regression by up to 12 AUC points under novel-antigen split — worth testing pairwise-ranking reformulation and pretraining data.
- CIR-DDG (arXiv 2608.27530) — bucket 2/3 — residual adapter w/ 22 geometric descriptors on SKEMPI Ab-Ag (343 complexes, 5-fold CV), improves 6 backbones by Spearman +0.03–0.13, RMSE -0.01 to -0.04 kcal/mol — directly matches ladder rung 8 (residual correction on FoldX), worth replicating the descriptor set.
- AbLWR (arXiv 2604.11272) — bucket 1+2 — 62,620 curated Ab-Ag pairs (8 sources incl. SKEMPI, SAbDab) w/ IgFold+ESM-2 fusion, PU-pretraining + listwise ranking beats regression baselines under Ag/Ab-held-out splits — candidate pretraining corpus and ranking-loss idea.
- ANDD unified antibody/nanobody dataset (Nature Sci Data, Feb 2026, s41597-026-06878-0) — bucket 1 — 48,683 sequences, 9,557 affinity values, 24,941 w/ structure, aggregates SKEMPI 2.0 among 15 sources — mutation-ΔΔG-specific coverage looks thin, lower priority than AbRank/AbLWR for augmentation.
- Scan ran, found 4 qualifying items (first run, no prior ledger).
## 2026-09-19
- USP-ddG (bioRxiv 2025.11.09.687124 / PMC13340161, code: github.com/ak422/USP-ddG) — bucket 2 — MoE fusion of inverse-folding + FoldX + geometric encoder, CATH-superfamily leakage-controlled split (813 held-out mutations, comparable rigor to our homology-cluster grouped CV), beats prior SoTA incl. +12.86%/10.78% Pearson/Spearman on CR6261-H1N1 antibody complex — strong candidate to replace/upgrade ladder rungs 6-7 (ESM-IF1/AntiFold + FoldX concatenation) with its MoE fusion and released code.
- AbAffinity chain-aware PLM (bioRxiv 2026.06.19.733375) — bucket 2 — frozen ESM-2 + trainable chain-aware gated cross-attention (separate H/L/antigen streams, CDR-focused pooling), only the compact interaction module is trained (cheap on modest GPU), few-shot with 30% labeled variants reaches Spearman 0.95 on select landscapes, evaluated incl. vs SKEMPI 2.0 — directly relevant fusion architecture and small-data evidence; worth trying as an alternative to naive late-fusion concatenation.
- Multi-scale fusion on AbAgym DMS data (bioRxiv 2026.06.09.730151) — bucket 2 — fuses physicochemical + ESM-2 + SASA/FoldX ΔΔG features (same modalities as our ladder) under leave-one-complex-out CV, explicit finding "only multi-scale fusion succeeds" (no single modality beats baseline) — direct empirical support for the project's late-fusion design; cite as justification.
- DSSA-PPI (PMC12758018) — bucket 2 — disentangled 5-way cross-attention (struct↔struct/seq/partner-seq) beats naive concatenation by PCC +0.03–0.04 on S1131 (1,131 mutations — our own dataset scale) under complex-level/LOCO leakage-controlled splits — alternative fusion mechanism to test in place of plain concatenation at rung 7.
- ProtAttBA (arXiv 2505.20301) — bucket 2/3 — sequence-only ESM-2 + cross-attention evaluated on S1131 (same underlying Ab-Ag SKEMPI subset as ours) under a 30%-seq-identity leakage-controlled split, PCC 0.41 on AB645 under that split vs. severe degradation for structure-based competitors — useful as an external comparison point/sanity check for our own homology-split numbers.
- Light-DDG / SKEMPI-Aug (arXiv 2502.06913) — bucket 1 — releases 670k pseudo-labelled mutations (SKEMPI v2 + Prompt-DDG-annotated augmentation), lightweight transformer + knowledge distillation, Pearson 0.544 per-structure — potential pretraining corpus for the sequence encoder, though pseudo-labels are not antibody-antigen-specific and quality depends on Prompt-DDG.
- AbBiBench (arXiv 2506.04235) — bucket 1/3 — 184,500 experimental measurements (Kd/enrichment from phage/yeast display DMS) across 14 Ab-Ag pairs / 9 antigens, zero-shot protocol correlating model likelihood with affinity — much larger real experimental resource than SKEMPI subset, but measurements are enrichment/Kd not ΔΔG so would need a conversion step to use as auxiliary pretraining signal.
- Scan ran, 7 qualifying items found.
## 2026-09-20
- de Kanter & Greiff, "Effects of protein interface mutations on protein quality and affinity" (bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.03.24.713863) — bucket 3 — new 7,185-mutation AlphaSeq VHH/IgG-antigen dataset (not yet public, "released upon approval") shows ESM-IF1 and ThermoMPNN correlate well on protein-quality (stability) mutants but significantly worse on true protein-interaction mutants (p=0.005; double-mutant r=0.44 vs single 0.59) — direct critique of ladder rungs 6-7 (ESM-IF1/AntiFold): these structure models may be reading stability, not binding; worth a quality-vs-interaction stratified error breakdown on our own residuals, and watch for the dataset release as an augmentation source.
- BindPred (Bioinformatics, btag309, online May 2026) — bucket 2 — PPB-Affinity corpus (11,919 complexes, SKEMPI v2+SAbDab+PDBbind+ATLAS, incl. Ab-Ag) w/ ESM2/MINT embeddings + GBT, PyRosetta/BindCraft structural features gave "minimal incremental benefit" over embeddings alone (PCC 0.86 random split, protein-level split held up reasonably) — counter-evidence to the project's fusion assumption at larger data scale; useful caution to cite when justifying why fusion is still worth trying in our much smaller regime (contrast w/ multi-scale-fusion and DSSA-PPI findings already logged 2026-09-19).
- Scan ran, 2 qualifying items found.
